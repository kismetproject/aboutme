Hi! My name is Dean. I'm an aspiring writer, big fan of supernatural, gothic, horror stuff, y'know. Spooky shit. Especially vampires. I am currently twenty-one years old. Big fan of Dr Pepper. Do not come to my town. Uhh also proshippers and darkshippers. I don't mind if you think toxic, unhealthy, or morally wrong dynamics are interesting from a writing type perspective, but interesting is the important word here. If you just like gooning to incest or shit like that, yeah no. DNI. I am very firm about this boundary for the sake of my wellbeing. Even if you're like "oh well I won't talk about it to YOU" no. Don't talk to me at all if you are one of those people.

Impala Metallica is a place for me to post shit, help people get to know me, and just is my space to be a weird dude lowkey.

about the site...

This site is deliberately small, gloomy and a little old-fashioned.

No algorithm. Just an archive that can grow slowly over time. Professionalism kind of takes a step back, because this is a space for me to show authenticity and let people see what I'm really like as a person.
